✨ Featured Expressive Word

Petrichor

/ˈpet.rɪ.kɔːr/ • noun

The pleasant, earthy scent produced when rain falls on dry soil after a long warm spell.

Instead of: "The nice smell after rain" Use: "The air was filled with fresh petrichor."
